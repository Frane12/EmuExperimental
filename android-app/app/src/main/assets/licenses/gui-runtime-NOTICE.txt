https://downloads.gamenative.app/imagefs_bionic.txz
SHA256: 368db62bfc58b72c97e5169bda9aa64d4246f07964c447e27c79a065e7e9c48b
Packaging recipe: tools/package_gui_runtime.py in Frane12/EmuExperimental.
Per-library checksums: gui-runtime-v1.zip / manifest.json.

Libraries are unmodified, independently dynamically loaded components. Only
X.Org X11 clients, XCB/Xau, android-sysvshm, FreeType, Fontconfig, Expat,
Brotli, bzip2, libpng, zlib and the Vulkan loader are included.
No GameNative proprietary redirect shim, Microsoft fonts, Wine binary or
GPU ICD is included in this bundle. Font discovery uses Android /system/fonts.
